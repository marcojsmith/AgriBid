# AgriBid design system

The single reference for how AgriBid looks and is built. Source of truth for values is `src/index.css`; for behaviour it is the components in `src/components/ui/` (shadcn primitives) and `src/components/ds/` (domain components). A live, dev-only reference page renders all of it: run `bun run dev` and open `/design-system` (not registered in production builds). Decision record: [ADR 0015](../../decisions/0015-design-system.md).

The confirmed design (updated 2026-11-23) comes from the page-by-page mockup track in `conductor/tracks/improve-screen-mockups/`:

- Owner decisions: [`decisions.md`](../../../conductor/tracks/improve-screen-mockups/decisions.md).
- Every component, every state, light and dark: [`rounds/components.html`](../../../conductor/tracks/improve-screen-mockups/rounds/components.html) (PNGs `components-375-*.png`, `components-375-dark-*.png`).
- Where the pages drew the same thing differently, and what to back-port: [`rounds/consistency-audit.md`](../../../conductor/tracks/improve-screen-mockups/rounds/consistency-audit.md).
- Pages: `rounds/home-r4.html`, `event-r2.html` (+ `event-r3.html` Home Screen sheet), `lot-r3.html`, `share-r2.html`.

The mockups are built from one component kit. Each kit component maps 1:1 to a component in `src/components/ds/`, and the table in section 5 says whether that component is built, designed, or needs changes. Where a value below differs from `src/index.css`, the mockup value is the confirmed design and `index.css` needs the change (status column "Changes").

Rules of use, in one line each:

- Use theme tokens, never hard-coded colours or fonts (`src/lib/theme-tokens.test.ts` fails on Tailwind palette classes).
- Every text/background pair must pass WCAG AA (4.5:1) in light and dark.
- Status is never shown by colour alone: always an icon or text too.
- Prefer flat sections and dividers to cards; never put a card in a card.
- Every word earns its place: no taglines, no decorative subtitles.

## 3. Spacing, radius, elevation

- **Spacing** follows a 4px rhythm. Page gutter 16px on phone; desktop container 1200px, centred, no padding. Section gap 20px on a lot page (`padding-top` 20 and `margin-top` 20 with a 1px divider), 24px on phone elsewhere, 32 to 40px on desktop. Card grid: 2 columns, gap 22px by 12px on phone; 3 columns, gap 36px by 24px on desktop. Header 56px high on phone, 68px on desktop.
- **Radius**: `rounded-sm` 4px (badges, pills, chips, lot chip, step numbers, quick-bid chips), `rounded-md` 6px (buttons, inputs, panels, banners, cost box, toast), `rounded-lg` 8px (images, avatars, sheet top corners). No `rounded-xl`/`rounded-2xl`. Pills are `rounded-sm`, not fully round. The signed-in avatar is the one circle.
- **Borders**: 1px `border`. A flat section is separated by a 1px divider, never by a card. The cost box and bid panel use a 1px border at 6px radius.
- **Elevation**: borders and dividers, not shadows. The only shadows are on floating layers: the watch heart on a photo (`0 1px 3px rgb(0 0 0 / .3)`) and the toast (`0 4px 16px rgb(0 0 0 / .3)`). Scrim behind a sheet: `rgb(0 0 0 / .5)`.
- **Tap targets**: 44px minimum below `md`. Buttons are 48px high (default) with 6px radius and 15px/600 text; `sm` is 40px and only appears in the desktop header. Chips, quick-bid chips, icon buttons, the WhatsApp icon button, back links and list links are 44px. Heart on a photo: 48px tap area around a 34px circle.
- **Sticky bar (phone)**: 68px high, `card` background, 1px top border, 16px sides. Left block (flex 1): title 14.5px/700 and sub 13px. Then a 44 by 44 WhatsApp icon button (1px border, 6px radius, 6px gap), then the CTA (48px high, 12px side padding). One layout, always in this order.
- **Bottom sheet**: `card` background, 8px top radius, padding 8px 16px 20px, grab bar 36 by 4px (2px radius, `border` colour), title row with 20px Lora title and a 44px close button, rows as 1px-divided definition list (term 14px muted, 118px wide).
- **Toast**: 16px from both sides, 24px from the bottom, `rgb(24 28 24 / .96)` background, white 15px text, 6px radius, padding 12px 14px, check icon.

## 4. Breakpoints

Mobile first. Unprefixed classes are the phone layout (design at 375x812). Then `md:` (768px, tablet) and `lg:` (1024px, desktop; check 1440x900). There are no custom breakpoints. The mockups are drawn at 375 and 1440 (1200px container).

## 5. Components

### shadcn primitives (`src/components/ui/`)

`Button` (variants incl. `default` using the `btn` token; sizes incl. `touch`), `Badge` (variants incl. status `live | soon | urgent | sold | passed`), `Input`, `Tabs`, `Card`. Use `Button` for actions and `asChild` with a `Link` for navigation. Use `Badge` for generic labels; use `StatusBadge` for auction and lot status. `Card` is rarely needed: prefer a flat section with a divider. Button default size changes from 44px to 48px on phone (see section 3).

Notes:

- `StatusBadge` and `StatusPill` are two shapes of one idea: a solid badge names a sale, a soft pill names a lot's reserve or outcome, and a `Banner` names the user's own state. Do not mix them.
- `Countdown` ticks every second; a `className` text colour (e.g. `text-white` on a photo overlay) overrides the tone colour. The older `CountdownTimer` is kept for existing callers and delegates to `Countdown`; new code uses `Countdown`.
- Pages that use `StickyBar` add bottom padding (about 5rem) to the page, and must keep the footer reachable. Standalone links have a 44px tap area on phone.
- `LotCard` states: sold shows "Sold for", not sold shows "Highest bid", under consideration shows "Top bid". Watch hearts appear only on open lots.
- Built in D1 (2026-11-23): everything marked Built (D1). Built in D5 (2026-11-23): `BackLink`, `LotNav`, `BidHistory`, `BidPanel`, `ConfirmBidSheet` (`src/components/ds`), `Gallery` and `GalleryViewer` (`src/components/lot/LotGallery`). Not built yet, left to the page work packages: `Header` states, `SortRow`, `BeforeYouBid`, `Footer`, `RemindMe`, `AddToHomeScreenSheet`, the 48px `Button`, and the `ConditionReport` / `FilterChips` / `SectionHeader` adoption of the new rows.

### Formatters

- `formatRand(n)` in `src/lib/currency.ts`: `R 200 000`, whole rand, no-break spaces (U+00A0) so a price never wraps (U+202F renders almost invisibly in Inter, giving "R250000"). Use it for display. `formatCurrency` keeps cents for statements.
- `formatSastDate(ms)` in `src/lib/dates.ts` returns `Wed 25 Nov, 11:00` (D1): no "SAST" (everything is South African time), fixed Africa/Johannesburg offset. `formatClosesIn(ms)` in `src/lib/countdown.ts` returns `Closes in 2d 3h`; seconds appear only in the final 10 minutes (`1m 52s`), 10 minutes to an hour shows minutes (`42m`).
- Fees: `calculateBuyerTotal` in `src/lib/vat.ts` is the single source of the maths. Premium is 12% of the bid (Admin-configurable); VAT is 15% of bid + premium for a VAT-registered seller (`vatBase: "bid-and-premium"`) and of the premium only for a private seller (`vatBase: "premium"`; `vatBaseForSeller` maps `vat | private | any`). R 400 000: VAT-registered seller pays R 515 200 (premium 48 000, VAT 67 200); private seller pays R 455 200 (premium 48 000, VAT 7 200). `CostBox` uses it.

## 6. Content rules

- **Money**: `R 200 000`, no cents. Labels: Opening bid, Current bid, Top bid, Sold for, Highest bid. Always show a worked total ("Bid R 400 000 → pay R 515 200").
- **VAT wording depends on the seller.** On a lot page: VAT-registered seller "12% premium + 15% VAT" with rows "Seller: Farming business · VAT registered" and "VAT: tax invoice issued"; private seller "12% premium + 15% VAT on the premium" with rows "Seller: Private farmer" and "No VAT on the bid (private seller)". On Home and Event, where the seller is not known: "12% premium + VAT · Fees & rules". The Fees & rules sheet explains it: "VAT is 15% on the premium. Also on the bid when the seller is VAT registered; each lot shows which."
- **Dates and times**: `Wed 25 Nov, 11:00`. No "SAST", no seconds, no ISO strings, no raw timestamps. Show a relative close time first ("Closes in 2d 3h") with the absolute time as secondary. Countdowns may show seconds under 10 minutes ("1m 52s").
- **Condition**: label every claim by its source: "Seller says: starts, runs and drives. No known faults." AgriBid does not inspect machines: never "Inspected by AgriBid". Disclaimer: "Sold as is. Book a viewing before you bid: 083 7 000 000" (tap to call).
- **Reserve**: before any bid "Reserve applies"; after bids "Reserve not met" or "Reserve met"; "No reserve". Explainer: "Reserve not met. If bidding ends below it, the seller decides whether to accept the top bid." After close below reserve the lot is "Under consideration" for 48 hours, then "Not sold".
- **After you win** ("If you win"): invoice by email, pay by EFT within 48 hours, "Your R 5 000 deposit counts towards payment.", collect within 7 days or we arrange transport.
- **Deposit**: "R 5 000 deposit, refunded within 3 working days if you don't buy" and "Have ready: ID + proof of address" (amounts and window are Admin settings).
- **No taglines**: no slogans, no decorative subtitles. Page titles and share text state facts ("AgriBid · Farm equipment auctions" is a name, not a tagline).
- **WhatsApp**: the green logo means "contact us" and nothing else, shown as an icon with an `aria-label`, never as a text label. Sharing uses a plain share glyph and the native share sheet (prefill: "{lot title} on AgriBid, closes {date}" + link). No floating buttons.
- **Fees & rules**: always named "Fees & rules" (footer link, cost box link, sheet title). The sheet links to "Read the full auction rules".
- **Reminders and alerts**: push only ("Remind me", "Watching. We'll notify you 1 hour before close and if you're outbid."). On iPhone Safari the Add to Home Screen sheet comes first. No SMS or email fallback.
- **Language**: plain South African English ("tyres", "colour", "metre"), "lots" for auction items. Afrikaans is out of scope. Say "about", not "est.". No jargon, no internal states, no database ids in the UI.
- **Labels**: sentence case. Buttons say what happens ("Register to bid", "Raise my bid", "Confirm bid R 417 500"). "Sell equipment" is the seller entry point. "Register to bid" is the filled call to action for guests, "My bids" for registered users.
- **Unknowns**: hide a row rather than show "N/A" or a made-up value (company registration number, VAT number, year, power). Legal details in the footer come from Admin settings only.
- **Numbers** that are shown as a count (lots, watching, bids) must match what the user can see.
