# Mockup rubric

Score each item 1–10 for the mockup board (`mockups.html`) across all three screens (Home, Auction event, Lot detail), at desktop and phone widths, in light and dark themes. Target: **≥ 9 on every item**.

A 9 means a senior product designer at a premium marketplace would ship it with at most cosmetic nits. A 10 is exemplary. Score the weakest screen/viewport, not the average.

| #   | Item                                | What a 9 looks like                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| --- | ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | **Visual hierarchy & scannability** | The eye lands on the right thing first on every screen (event status/timing, lot price + countdown, primary CTA). Clear type scale, no competing emphasis, consistent grouping.                                                                                           |
| 2   | **Brand & premium feel**            | Uses AgriBid tokens (forest green, warm neutrals, Lora headings, Inter UI with tabular figures). Restrained: radius 4–6px on UI, 8px only on images; no card-in-card; feels like a serious R200k+ marketplace, not a SaaS template.                                       |
| 3   | **Trust & transparency**            | Fees, VAT, reserve, payment, collection, viewing, seller verification and bid record are visible where the decision happens. Nothing empty, fake-looking, internal (DB ids, admin states) or contradictory.                                                               |
| 4   | **Conversion clarity**              | Every lot/state has one obvious next action; no dead ends; state-correct CTAs (guest / registered pre-start / live); sticky mobile bar always agrees with the panel.                                                                                                      |
| 5   | **Mobile UX (375px)**               | Mobile-first: correct content order, tap targets ≥ 44px, no horizontal overflow or clipping, readable sizes, thumb-reachable primary action, filters/sort usable.                                                                                                         |
| 6   | **Desktop layout & space**          | Grids aligned to the header edge, sensible column counts, no large dead areas, sticky panels behave, density right for 1440px.                                                                                                                                            |
| 7   | **Imagery**                         | Uses the real seed equipment photos (`assets/`), consistent aspect ratios (16:9 events, 4:3 lots/gallery), good cropping (`object-fit`), photos lead the lot page, gallery reads as an inspection tool. No mismatched photo/lot pairs (a Case photo on a John Deere lot). |
| 8   | **Copy & microcopy**                | Plain, specific South African English; consistent labels (Opening bid / Current bid / Sold for); ZAR as "R 200 000"; dates "Sat 22 Nov, 10:00 SAST"; no jargon, no test-data copy.                                                                                        |
| 9   | **Accessibility & theming**         | WCAG AA contrast in light AND dark; semantic structure; visible focus; status never conveyed by colour alone; dark mode looks designed, not inverted.                                                                                                                     |
| 10  | **Craft & polish**                  | Pixel-level alignment, consistent spacing rhythm, consistent components across screens, no clipping/wrapping bugs, no console errors, board chrome itself tidy.                                                                                                           |

Review output format: per item `score/10 — one-line justification`, then a numbered fix list ordered by score impact, each `screen · viewport · problem → exact fix`.
